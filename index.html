HOW IT WORKS

Skin in the game.
Money back on losses.

A picks marketplace where sellers stake their own capital — so you don't have to gamble on whether they're real.

1

Sellers post a pick AND stake their own money

Every listing requires the seller to put up capital from their stake balance. They can't sell picks they don't believe in.

2

You buy the pick — payment held in escrow

PicksVault holds your payment until the game settles. Pick details unlock immediately so you can place your bet.

3

Game ends → official box score auto-grades it

No human review. We pull final stats from the official feed within an hour of the final whistle.

4

Win → seller paid. Loss → you refunded.

If the pick hits, seller gets paid (minus 5% fee). If it loses, you get 95% back automatically and the seller's stake is slashed.

Why this works

Traditional pick sellers profit by selling to large audiences regardless of accuracy. PicksVault's stake mechanism makes that economically impossible — losing sellers run out of stake and are forced off the platform. Only sellers with a real edge can sustain selling here.

Edge cases, handled.

Real sportsbooks have rules for messy outcomes. We mirror them so the marketplace stays predictable.

⏸️

Postponed game

Pick is rolled to the rescheduled game automatically. If the game isn't played within 7 days, full refund to buyer + stake returned to seller.

↔️

Push (exact line)

Total bases of exactly 1.5? Impossible. But for whole-number lines, a push refunds the buyer 100% with no stake slash for the seller. Counts as N/A in their record.

🩹

Player scratched / DNP

If the player doesn't appear in the game, the pick voids. Full buyer refund, stake returned, no rating impact.

📉

Line moved drastically

If the market line moves >10% from the seller's quoted line before the pick locks, buyers get a 1-click cancel option. Seller can void and re-list.

⚖️

Disputed grade

Box scores disagree? Stat correction post-game? We use the official league feed as source of truth, with a 24h dispute window for both sides to flag obvious errors.

🚫

Suspected collusion

A seller can't buy their own picks. Sellers need a minimum buyer count + IP/device diversity before stakes are slashed. Pattern detection flags wash trading.
